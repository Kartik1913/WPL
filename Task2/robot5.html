Mars Rover

A Mars Rover is a robotic vehicle designed to explore the surface of Mars. NASA has developed several rovers to study the planet's geology, climate, and potential for past life.

Key Features

  • Remote operation
  • Scientific instruments
  • Rock and soil analysis
  • High-resolution cameras
  • Solar or nuclear power systems

Applications

  1. Space exploration
  2. Geological research
  3. Climate studies
  4. Search for evidence of past life
