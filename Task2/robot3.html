HRP-4C Robot

HRP-4C is a humanoid robot developed in Japan. It is designed with a realistic human appearance and can perform walking, speaking, and dancing.

Key Features

  • Human-like appearance
  • Speech capability
  • Facial expressions
  • Walking and dancing
  • Motion control

Applications

  1. Entertainment
  2. Research
  3. Exhibitions
  4. Human-robot interaction studies

Developer

National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), Japan
